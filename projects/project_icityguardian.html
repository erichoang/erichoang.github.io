iCityGuardian / Refer-CityFlow & M3Track

Language-guided, human-interactive multi-camera AI, covering natural-language target retrieval, cross-camera object association, and benchmarking of multimodal video understanding.

This line of research at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga (UTC) studies how people can find and follow targets across many city cameras by describing them in natural language. It brings together two related but distinct efforts.

Refer-CityFlow and M3Track: research benchmark and reference framework

Refer-CityFlow is a benchmark dataset for language-driven multi-target multi-camera tracking, and M3Track is a reference framework evaluated on it. The benchmark connects natural-language queries with multi-object, multi-camera video trajectories:

  • 40 cameras
  • more than 660 cross-camera trajectories
  • 4,362 trajectory descriptions
  • 7,699 natural-language queries

The paper Refer-CityFlow and M3Track: A Benchmark Dataset and Reference Framework for Language-Driven Multi-Target Multi-Camera Tracking has been accepted at WACV 2027 (see publications). The benchmark is intended for evaluating research methods in multimodal video understanding; results on it are research evaluations, not measures of product readiness.

iCityGuardian: research and technology-translation initiative

iCityGuardian explores human-interactive, real-time multi-camera tracking for smart-city safety and mobility. It studies natural-language-guided multi-camera retrieval, cross-view association, and operator-facing workflows in which people describe targets in natural language. Related invention: human-interactive, real-time multi-camera tracking (patent pending; see patents).
