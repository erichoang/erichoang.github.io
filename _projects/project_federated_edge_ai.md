This research at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga (UTC), carried out in a UTC–DENSO research collaboration, studies how roadside sensing systems at many urban intersections can learn together without centralizing raw data.

## Research themes

- **Personalized federated learning:** models that share knowledge across intersections while adapting to each site's traffic patterns.
- **Multi-agent trajectory forecasting:** predicting the future motion of road users at intersections.
- **Decentralized, privacy-conscious learning:** keeping raw sensor data at the edge and exchanging model updates instead.
- **Edge deployment and real-world evaluation:** research pipelines toward running forecasting models on roadside edge hardware and evaluating them under real-world conditions.

## FLEET

FLEET is an ongoing research framework in this project for real-time, edge-deployable personalized federated learning for trajectory forecasting. Related inventions are patent pending (see [patents](/patents/)). Publications from this work will be listed on the [publications](/publications/) page as they appear.
