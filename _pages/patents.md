Dr. Nguyen is the **lead inventor** on four distinct patent-pending invention families in human-interactive computer vision, personalized federated learning, edge AI, and smart-contract security: three U.S. nonprovisional applications and one separate PCT application. One of the U.S. applications also has a corresponding PCT filing, which belongs to the same invention family and is not counted as an additional invention. The entries below are **patent applications**, not granted patents. Ownership and inventorship are governed by the corresponding official filings.

**Role:** Lead Inventor  
**Applications:** U.S. Application No. 19/857,895; corresponding PCT/US26/53548
