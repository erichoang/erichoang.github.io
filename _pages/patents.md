My research includes patent-pending work in human-interactive computer vision, edge AI, federated learning, and smart-contract security. The entries below describe **patent applications and related research IP**, not granted patents. Ownership and inventorship are governed by the corresponding official filings.

## Human-interactive multi-camera tracking

**iCityGuardian: Human-Interactive Multi-Camera Tracking for Smart Cities**  
**Status:** U.S. patent pending; provisional application **63/908,882**.  
Research collaboration: University of Tennessee at Chattanooga and University of Arkansas. The system uses natural-language interaction and multi-camera tracking for smart-city safety and mobility applications.

- [UTC research overview](https://www.utc.edu/research/center-for-urban-informatics-and-progress/research/cuip-ai-research)
- [UTC faculty research feature](https://blog.utc.edu/news/2026/06/behind-the-research-at-utc-dr-eric-hoang-h-nguyen/)

## Federated learning and edge AI for transportation

**FLEET: Real-Time Edge-Deployable Personalized Federated Learning for Multi-Agent Trajectory Forecasting**  
**Status:** U.S. patent pending.  
A UTC–DENSO research collaboration on privacy-preserving, decentralized trajectory forecasting and end-to-end edge deployment.

**Personalized Federated Learning for Multi-Agent Trajectory Forecasting**  
**Status:** U.S. patent pending.  
A UTC–DENSO research collaboration on learning across distributed roadside sensing systems without centralizing raw data.
