Human-interactive multi-camera tracking

iCityGuardian: Human-Interactive Multi-Camera Tracking for Smart Cities
Status: U.S. patent pending; provisional application 63/908,882.
Research collaboration: University of Tennessee at Chattanooga and University of Arkansas. The system uses natural-language interaction and multi-camera tracking for smart-city safety and mobility applications.
